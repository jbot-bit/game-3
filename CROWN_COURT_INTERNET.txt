CROWN COURT 0.17.0 - FAMILY INTERNET PLAY

WINDOWS
Download CrownCourt.exe and double-click. No ZIP, installer, engine or
configuration file is needed. Use the current download on each PC.
If the blue warning says "Windows protected your PC", click the small
"More info" text link below its message, then "Run anyway" for this game.
If those options are absent, keep the exact warning text for diagnosis;
Smart App Control and Microsoft Store restrictions use different screens.
If updating from the old internet ZIP, put this EXE in a new folder.
Do not leave the old internet.cfg beside it.

MAC
Download CrownCourt.dmg. Open it, drag Crown Court to Applications,
then open Crown Court from Applications. No Terminal commands needed.
The app contains both Intel and Apple-silicon binaries.
Native Apple-silicon startup and internet joining passed. Intel graphical
startup failed on the test VM; an unknown iMac is not signed off.
This family app is ad-hoc signed, not Apple notarized. After attempting
to open it, use System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway if
macOS blocks an unidentified developer. Do not disable Gatekeeper.
If macOS reports malware or a damaged app, stop and report that message.

PLAY TOGETHER - NO CODES OR IP ADDRESSES
1. Everyone opens the current game and enters their name.
2. Dad chooses Host online game.
3. Kids choose Find online game, then click Dad's name.
4. Dad waits for all three names, chooses teams/bots, then Start.
5. Keep Dad's game open. Rematches stay in the same room.
The list refreshes automatically. A full or started game is not listed.
Open games are visible to other players. Same Wi-Fi / LAN is only for
playing on one home network, not between your two houses.

SERVER AND SAVES
The independent Sydney server is live at direct-metro.bnr.la. No tunnel
or background relay on Dad's PC is needed. Its service restarts after
a process failure/reboot. Saved progress stays on each player's computer;
it is not on this relay server. Do not delete your saves when updating.

IF JOINING FAILS
Confirm everyone downloaded the current version. Dad returns to the menu
and hosts again; kids choose Find online game again. Keep the exact text
of any connection error for diagnosis.
Health check: https://direct-metro.bnr.la/healthz
A healthy relay does not prove an individual Mac can render the game.
