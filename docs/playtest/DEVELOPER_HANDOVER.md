# Hands Full — quick handover

**The game:** four selectable modes on Windows LAN. Knockout is circular last-player-standing with escalating sweepers and a shrinking physical pad. Gladiator is a padded-stick ring-out match for two or three players, first to three wins; LMB swings and RMB braces. Wipeout is a130m race for one to three players with two tipping beams, two physical sweepers, four jump podiums and three ordered checkpoints. Co-op preserves twenty approach layouts followed by mandatory three-person bridge/lift/cargo stages. Blank people, fast movement and earned cosmetics carry across modes. Hands and grapples remain in Co-op; competitive modes use equal movement without powers.

**Progress:** pick one of three random powers. A successful clear saves the powers everyone used and one random three-card cosmetic bag. Common60%, rare28%, epic10%, legendary2% per card; new items are preferred within a rolled tier. Fifteen paints/accessories, animated foil reveals, optional blank appearance. The host owns the crew collection and per-slot looks. Atomic save/retry keeps a pending roll stable and duplicate completions cannot roll again.

**Start:** `build/hands-full-0.8.0/HandsFull.exe`. Matching package: `build/HandsFull-0.8.0-windows.zip`. Pick a mode and host; Esc shows the join address. The other PCs use this same build and join it. Leave to the menu to switch mode. Keep the same host for shared cosmetics. The0.6.0 package remains separate. Full controls: `prototypes/physics_party/README.md`.
