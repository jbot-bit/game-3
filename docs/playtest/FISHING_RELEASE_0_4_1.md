# One More Cast 0.4.1

The largest clarity problem in the previous build was the disappearance of the next-step card after the first rod upgrade. The remaining boat and beacon adventure was only explained in the journal. The guide now continues through the essential parts, fishing income, banking, repairs, and the night escape. It derives every step from existing campaign progress; it adds no quest state, extra controls or mandatory rod equip.

Two smaller input and fairness problems are also fixed. A carried whole fish now displays the existing hold-to-pack key alongside Drop and Throw, including remapped keys. Nearby night shelters show their real remaining power. The final 30-second warning survives catch messages and catch reveals; an active chase reports actual protection, and an expired shelter directs the player toward Moth. These messages use the same authoritative safety rules as the hunter. Outside a powered circle, an active threat explicitly says to move inside its light.

The outing review also exposed a lantern timing bug: facing the hunter during its stationary warning spent the slowdown and cooldown before the chase. The effect now starts during the chase. The two-second effect, eight-second cooldown, hunter speed, shelter duration and fish values are unchanged.
