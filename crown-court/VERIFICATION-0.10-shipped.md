# Crown Court 0.10.0 verification

Windows, 12 October 2026. Godot 4.7.2 Compatibility renderer. The prior 0.7 release remains available; 0.8 and 0.9 are held comparison builds. Sibling games and player saves are preserved. The new arena follows retained public full-match images and an official overhead. Its dimensions and physics are authored, not extracted private Crown Jam values.

## Arena and gameplay changes

The 64x112 court now has broad winding roads, three-metre crowns, bowed crossings, low turf pockets and curved goal approaches. A three-tier curved stadium, fascia ribbons, team end shells, original large mascots, emblems and launcher surrounds replace the blank horizon. Boosters, sweepers, scoring openings and ordinary movement/jump constants remain intact.

The first terrain pass exposed a tight bank that lifted a running player off the ground and incorrectly facing render triangles. Both were repaired. Selective mesh refinement reduced the worst sampled triangle-centroid discrepancy from 0.418m to 0.04345m; all vertices agree with the shared physical heightfield within 0.000051m. Native tests inspect real crowd instance transforms, new scenery footprints and camera visibility. Headless crowd transform readback is unavailable and is not used for full clearance claims.

Bots now compare a loose ball's height with their own feet. In the reproduced bank case, this prevents jumping away from a lower ball and reduces pickup time from 1.217s to 0.267s. Elevated catches still jump normally. The 20 terrain regressions cover grounded traversal, bank rebounds, launchers, both actual dunks, unchanged ordinary jump apex and conservative shot-forecast bounds.

The existing predictive passing, continuous charged shots, physical rim/backboard scoring, Hype/assist lifecycle, audio-event handling, controller inputs, wardrobe/rewards and network recovery carry forward. New source tests correct only demonstrated obsolete fixtures: shots now start at the actual terrain height, and a speed-strip checkpoint measures grounded height relative to the floor.

## Bugs found by complete-match review

The first 0.10 candidate remains held as before evidence at build/crown-court-evidence/v10-dunk-held-baseline/CrownCourt.exe (SHA256 0B601D55B3EC358404463A536443DD7EAAC575B321DAB83DF252CA3F4D0D9930). Its completed recordings exposed two bugs that the original fixed-aim fixtures missed. REVIEW-0.10-MATCHES.md retains their diagnosis, recordings and failed reproductions.

First, changing aim during a dunk moved the hands and release sideways while the body followed its original committed path. Six uncancelled local dunks missed in the old room match. The repair freezes the dunk axis for body, hands and physical release until the animation finishes. The changing-input regression improved from 32/64 on the old executable to 64/64 on the final executable, covering both hoops, four approach directions and normal/Giant players. Presentation alignment passes nine further checks.

Second, a still-grounded network snapshot could extrapolate beyond a raised kickoff deck and project the character down to the distant floor before the next airborne update. Real simulation/packet/presentation reproduction measured a 5.071m target drop and 3.224m visible dip. Preserving height across this loss of elevated support reduces the worst target step to 0.141m and removes the erroneous dip. All 27 checks pass, including local/remote exits, both ends, held-ball coupling, bank contact, pending jumps and underneath-deck behavior.

## Limits

The denser stadium has a measured cost. In the paired controlled source views, scene construction increased from 2.91s to 5.94s, draw calls from 931 to 1397, and presentation-component CPU p95 from 0.798ms to 1.005ms. Those are uncontrolled single-run component observations, not matched GPU or whole-match performance claims. MovieWriter's fixed clock is also not a performance benchmark. Construction happens once: a separate audit confirmed that later menu/lobby/match build calls reuse the existing World, with 30 calls at 0.084ms median / 0.126ms p95 and unchanged terrain identity. No redundant cache was added.

Physical mixed controllers, speaker/headphone preference, separate PCs, human enjoyment and real cross-home smoothness remain unverified. A local three-client run or delayed transport fixture is not a household/WAN test. The public WSS endpoint is blank, so no-extra-app Internet play is not live. No public deployment, router/firewall change or external publication is included.

For disk capacity, redundant raw AVI files from the 0.6, 0.7, held 0.9, first 0.10 and final 0.10 offline matches, plus selected raw JPEGs from the held 0.8 and first 0.10 room recordings, were removed after complete encoded-video decode and hash verification. Complete original MP4s, timestamps, telemetry, selected raw frames, reviews and raw-file hash manifests remain. The final room's raw JPEGs remain intact. Game builds and packages were not removed.
