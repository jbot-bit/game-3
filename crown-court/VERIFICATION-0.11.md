## Gameplay and visual evidence

All 52 embedded regression scripts pass against the final x64 executable. Six gameplay/input/terrain/presentation suites pass on the x86 Godot VM with its final embedded pack. Native x64 gates pass 37 arena, 18 input, 14 audio and 65 controller-event checks; the audio test observes 32 actual voices. The normal-input pickup/boost/launch/extra-jump/dunk/physical-score route completes with one kickoff reset. Controller events establish software behavior, not physical mixed-controller detection.

Ordinary human shots retain the actual 3D aim through preview, input RPC and launch. The old six-degree off-target shot was corrected toward the basket; the new shot retains six degrees. Tests cover yaw, charge and independence from basket range, plus actual physical baskets at both ends. Bot aim alone uses imperfect ballistic targeting. Easy bots wait and wind up longer than Normal/Hard; possession changes and takeover clear cached decisions and charge. A review also found and fixed charging that could survive a missed possession-loss/recatch snapshot.

Tackles now impart an 11.5m/s horizontal impulse and 5.2m/s upward impulse, with a visible recovery interval. The retained reproduction reaches about 0.633m peak height, versus 0.33m previously. Its total displacement includes subsequent attacker contact, so it is not described as pure impulse travel. Dunks reach and release sooner while preserving the fixed body/hand/ball axis; changing-aim checks still pass 64/64 and nine presentation checks pass.

The court keeps its 64x112 bounds, launch pads, speed strips and sweepers. Main raised roads are 1.15m high with narrower shoulders and broad low turf; physical and visible terrain share one layout. The measured maximum sampled facet-centroid discrepancy is 0.03925m. Native arena clearance, front-face and sightline checks pass. Game Development Studio sealed nine matched old/new camera views, verified hashes and compared all nine without missing captures. This establishes comparable visual changes, not original-game equivalence or performance improvement. See ARENA-0.11.md and REVIEW-0.11.md.
