# Runtime interfaces

0.10 replaces the shallow rectangular terrain with shared scalar winding-road and bank helpers. Road crowns are 3m above the low turf, 6m wide with 2.4m shoulders; bowed cross routes and front goal loops connect the flanks. Simulation, forecast bounds, player prediction, world mesh and markings must agree on that heightfield. Stadium scenery stays outside the playable boundary and cannot imply invisible interior collision. Arena dimensions, goal positions, normal movement/jump constants and physical scoring stay unchanged from 0.9. ARENA-0.10.md records the authored layout and visual reference.

0.9 supersedes earlier range/dimension values: playable court64x112, hoops96m apart, pass range70m and flight0.3-2.6s. `movement_velocity` is shared by live movement and full-flight receiver forecasting. Shots smoothly blend assistance with charge/range/aim and remain ballistic/interceptable. `secure_ball` atomically sets position/ownership/velocity. A real completed pass records an eight-second assist opportunity, cleared by other possession, tackle/hazard drop, score or reset; a qualifying basket awards20 Hype once. No private original-game constants are claimed.

`HAZARDS`, `hazard_angle` and `hazard_endpoints` define four padded sweepers. State.clock drives both rendering and contact; no separate client clock advances them while paused/reset. Their bodies are jumpable, physically rebound balls, and briefly knock/drop ordinary players with a1.1s per-player retrigger guard. Giant players ignore these ground hazards. Sweepers cannot generate tackle stats or Hype. Canonical arena constants drive terrain, world meshes, prediction bounds, kickoff and bot outlets.

0.6 additions: successful active/passive pass receptions share one transaction and Hype credit. Team passes choose an unstunned, nondunking teammate within 34 metres and a forward 120-degree cone; no target leaves possession intact. Bot receive cooldowns affect decisions only, never explicit human actions. One loose-ball collector and two moving support lanes replace the shared ball chase. HUD score announcements follow authoritative score changes, independently of the latest event. Headless presentation is disabled by default while HUD/result/reward contracts remain active; explicit visual diagnostics may opt back in.

Godot 4.7.2. Scripts use preloads and Variant dictionaries. sim.gd constants are the canonical dimensions and tuning.

## Simulation

Transient feedback uses `recent_events`: at most 16 tuples `[id, kind, position, source_player, event_clock]`, expiring after 350 ms. `event_clock` advances even during celebration/result freeze and resets per round; history clears on reset. IDs remain monotonic. Snapshots deep-copy history. Consumers seed on join/new round, consume each ID once, discard expired/invalid records and advance silently while muted/paused. Latest-event fields remain available to existing visual effects. Local sound priority cannot increase the ten-voice budget or preempt reserved score/announcement voices. Charge input belongs to one possession and cancels on ownership loss; a held trigger must be released before another charge begins.

sim.gd extends RefCounted. reset(mode, setup={}) accepts coop, teams, or versus. Custom setup contains active_humans, teams (seat to side), and bots (side to count). Human seats are 1-3. Co-op uses six players; custom teams support up to three per side; free-for-all uses three individual scoring teams.

move_input(id,direction,aim) accepts horizontal Vector3s. action(id,command,charge=0.5) handles jump, catch/grab, throw, pass, tackle, dunk, request_pass and hype. step(dt) runs at 60 Hz on the host. bot_command(id) returns move, aim, action and charge; the application applies it only to seats without a connected human. add_human(id,side) can replace a same-side bot, including possession and active power references.

snapshot() deep-copies players, ball, scores, mode, clock, time_left, winner, reset_time, match_setup, power_pads, power_balls, events and action counters. Player positions are feet; each player includes team, pos, vel, facing, move, aim, grounded, stun, guard, dive, jump_count, dunk, charge, hype and power. Ball contains pos, vel, owner (0 means loose), last_touch, lock and pass_target.

The banked court is x +/-20, z +/-36. Hoops are at (0,4.6,+/-31); ball radius is .52. Reuse floor_height, surface_height, floor_normal, hand_position, hoop, can_dunk, can_catch, booster_direction, throw_velocity and launch_velocity instead of duplicating formulas. Broad side and crossing ridges share the same floor function. Teams start on one-way rear decks at z +/-34, y 7.5; surface_height admits contact from above without creating an invisible wall underneath. Countdown launches players toward midfield with one extra jump available. Live joins choose an open spawn without resetting existing players. PADS launch; SPEED_PADS accelerate grounded players. Player boost_time/boost_direction/boost_speed and buffered jump/dunk timers are authoritative.

Shots and dunks score through the same downward rim-crossing test. Each dunk has dunk_duration, dunk_start, dunk_target, dunk_velocity and dunk_reach; presentation uses its actual duration. Ordinary dunks last 1.5 seconds: reach ends at phase .4 and release occurs at .8. High approaches extend duration for continuous motion. The body and held-ball height blend together into the rim pose. Team games use five minutes/four-point lead; free-for-all uses three minutes/seven points. Ties go to sudden death. A three-second countdown applies at kickoff and after baskets; the first .85 seconds after a score preserve the dunk/landing scene through snapshot celebration_time. Rewards are eligible immediately; the result overlay waits for that celebration to finish.

## Presentation

Grounded unboosted movement and local prediction share Sim.ground_velocity. Horizontal acceleration is a vector budget, independent of world axes; turn slip does not disable input or change air/boost impulses. World stride/lean may use intent versus velocity but never feeds back into physics.

Loose-ball network corrections are bounded presentation offsets and stop decaying when prediction reaches its age cap. Ownership changes, round/reset changes and large relocations clear them. World catch gather lasts 0.10 seconds and shares one ball target with both hands; quick releases remain continuous. These are presentation contracts, not extra possession or scoring windows.

world.gd extends Node3D. build(mode) creates the original court and actors; present(state,local_id,dt,yaw,pitch) updates them and the follow camera. Optional state fields include names, cosmetics, camera_focus and pass_target_preview. Simulation owns all collisions. world.camera is available for rendering checks. Shared simulation geometry also drives the banked mesh, trajectory display and dunk animation. Clients extrapolate a bounded visual copy, including both jumps and the ball; the held ball and hands additionally share the interpolated actor root offset. Presentation never writes authoritative state.

## Application and network

main.gd owns menu/lobby/match/connecting phases, input, wardrobe, ENet, room transport and audio. Public flow methods are practice_game(mode), host_game(mode), join_game(address), host_room(mode), join_room(code), start_match(), close_session(message=""), submit_move(direction,aim) and submit_action(command,charge=0.5). manual_input_enabled permits the same command path to be driven by integration tests.

room_peer.gd extends MultiplayerPeerExtension over WebSocketPeer. configure(url, create_room, code, game_version) remains connecting until registration is acknowledged; room_ready and room_error report the result. Adjacent internet.cfg supplies the service URL. Public URLs require wss; ws is restricted to literal loopback. The service limits a room to host plus two clients, validates identity and routes clients only to their host. Godot channel identifiers are retained, but all packets share TCP delivery; an impaired stream can stall actions. See INTERNET-DESIGN-0.5.md and relay/README.md for protocol and queue limits. A blank URL disables Internet room controls without affecting practice or LAN. A working local relay is not proof of deployed public connectivity.

seats maps peer ID to human seat. The host owns team choices, optional bot balancing and all physics. UDP port 28888 supports host plus two clients. Movement and compressed snapshots up to 1200 bytes use unreliable ordered transport; roster, actions, power selection and match transitions are reliable. Larger snapshots use reliable channel 4, with at most four awaiting acknowledgement per peer; when full, later large samples are discarded until an acknowledgement frees a slot, then current state is sent. Small snapshots continue independently. A per-round sequence rejects stale updates across both snapshot channels. Reliable delivery is acknowledged using the packet's own round and sequence even if it precedes begin_match; the host validates both against that peer's pending sequences. Each action carries its current aim and movement direction. Both input RPCs include match_id and reject an old round. Catch/pass intent resolves at the pressing client, so unseen possession changes cannot convert a catch into an unintended pass. Stale client movement stops after .35 seconds. A disconnected seat is controlled by a bot. All PCs must run the same version.

Each round has a unique match_id. At the final whistle the host freezes eligible peer IDs. A winning peer receives one locally saved bag per round; a new connection cannot inherit the previous occupant's reward. Failed saves remain in the application's pending queue and retry while it is open. profile.gd owns atomic save/backup, reward deduplication, owned-item validation and nonduplicate rarity draws.

Keyboard/mouse: WASD move, mouse look, Space double jump, LMB catch/pass, RMB tackle/charge-and-release shot, F airborne dunk, E explicit catch, Shift explicit tackle, Ctrl ball camera, MMB request pass, Q selected Hype, Esc/F1 pause/controls. See README for controller bindings.
