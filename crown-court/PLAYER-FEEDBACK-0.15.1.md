## Why this scope

The selected changes address an observed local proportion mismatch and a documented discoverability complaint without rewriting progression or adding unrelated systems. The movement/camera fixes in the accompanying review documents come from reproduced local defects, not assumptions that every online complaint applies here. The smaller Fresh Bean remains visually shorter: 5.8 is approximately 3.4 bean heights versus 2.7 family heights. Shared collision, jump and dunk rules remain unchanged across cosmetic identities.

## Evidence and remaining review

Native review of the raised rim revealed a second issue: from 5m away the default camera placed the rim behind the scoreboard. The final 0.15.2 camera blends in nearby facing-hoop framing, preserving manual yaw and ball-camera behaviour. At 1280x800 the controlled rim top moves from 69px to 168px while player feet remain at 666px; approach, departure and turning-away checks pass. This follow-up came from inspecting the game, not from assuming the proportion change was complete after physics tests.

Before the height/support fix, five focused proportion/connection checks failed; after it, all six passed. Existing 64 dunk alignment checks, 15 shot checks, passing/interception, tackle and open-basket geometry checks passed at the raised hoop. The full gameplay gate then exposed two 5m test shots clipping the raised rim: their old 70-degree aim was too shallow. The deliberate-shot fixture now uses 78 degrees inside 6m, within the existing 80-degree human limit; both directions pass without changing gameplay aim, charge or ball physics. Twenty contextual-hint checks cover keyboard/controller labels and unavailable-action suppression. Final packaged and ordinary-input receipts are maintained in [VERIFICATION.md](VERIFICATION.md).

The next useful household comparison is whether the higher hoop improves jump/dunk readability and whether defenders understand the counter without coaching. Actual enjoyment, physical controllers, separate home PCs and WAN remain distinct from automated local verification.
