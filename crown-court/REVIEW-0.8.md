# 0.8 recorded-play debugging review

12 October 2026. The input is the retained 0.7 release recordings and the user's movement, sound and camera feedback; no new household recording was attached. The complete 0.7 source manifest was preserved in build/crown-court-evidence/v8-before-source before production edits. Prior executables and ZIPs remain intact.

## Reproduced repairs

- **Lost action sounds:** a real local throw followed by an opponent jump before one HUD update played only the jump. A bounded history now delivers multiple accepted actions once, with local priority, nearby catches and silence for distant activity. It retains at most 16 compact entries for 350 ms. Join/rematch, pause/mute, stale entries and duplicate/malformed tuples are covered. Independent review also found that a remote jump in the previous frame could consume the local jump's cooldown; priority now covers that case. Local cues may replace an ambient impact voice in a full pool, while the total voice count and reserved score/countdown voices remain fixed. The existing 21-sample bank is retained.
- **Stale charged shots:** holding shoot through possession loss and a recatch retained the previous charge and fired on release. The charge now cancels when ownership loss is observed, while the held-button latch prevents an automatic restart. Seven parsed-mouse input checks went from four failures to all passing; a fresh press/release still shoots once. This is not physical-controller evidence.
- **Network presentation:** actual compressed receive-state probes against the 0.7 executable reproduced a .1067 m carrier target rewind, .0272 m rendered actor reversal, and large actor/ball cuts after a 525 ms delivery gap. Per-player correction now keeps the carrier and held ball together. Longer corrections are admitted only when consecutive authoritative samples agree with continuous physical motion within 600 ms; resets, inconsistent relocations and action transitions retain immediate handling. Catch-up is bounded, then retires to current motion. Additional 30/120 Hz review exposed a prediction-cap boundary that lost motion at 30 Hz, and a banked-ground contact edge; both received focused regressions and repairs. This smooths particular recovery transitions; it cannot eliminate network stalls.
- **Hoop obstruction:** the retained rear-rim view hid the torso on 9/9 probe rays and the ball on 8/9. Foreground rim/net sections now fade independently against the rendered torso and ball sightlines. Both obstruction counts become zero while 62.5% of rim triangles remain solid in that view. Clear approaches, normal/Giant carry and backboard intersections are covered. Camera transforms, ball/player physics and scoring geometry are unchanged. Splitting visibility sections adds 56 small geometry draws; no hardware performance improvement is claimed.

Signal integrity is not an audition or a claim that the user likes the sound.
