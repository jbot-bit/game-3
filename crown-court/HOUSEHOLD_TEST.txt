CROWN COURT - CONTROLLER AND FRIENDS MATCH

Use the current 0.17.0 CrownCourt.exe on Windows or CrownCourt.dmg on Mac.
Host online game; Find online game; click the host name; then Start.
No codes or IP addresses. Same Wi-Fi / LAN is for one home network only.
See INTERNET_PLAY.txt for current Mac verification limits.

Before playing, connect your controller. Navigate from the menu using
the D-pad/stick and A/Cross. Enter a name/address using the controller
keyboard. Use B/Circle to go back. Confirm the on-screen labels match.

Set Easy bots first. Try Normal/Hard later; joining players should see the
host selection. Test aimed misses: aim beside the hoop and confirm the
ball follows the preview without steering back. Charge changes distance.
Take a tackle and check the lift, push and recovery on both PCs.

1. Choose 3 vs bots. With all three people connected, start the match.
   There should be three humans in blue and three bots in orange.
   Each person should run, double jump, catch, pass, shoot, tackle and dunk.
   Try a pass to a moving teammate and a dunk while an opponent challenges.
   Take a round launch pad, release movement briefly, then use the extra
   jump. It should keep its forward travel and climb, rather than brake.
   Cross a striped speed pad, follow the winding road and cut over a bank.
   Try the low goal pocket and raised approach from both sides. Watch for
   feet or balls sinking into the surface and scenery hiding the rim.
   Try jump then immediately F: the dunk should wait for takeoff, reach
   the outside of the rim, slam, and land before the kickoff reset.
   Turn the camera during a dunk: your hands and ball should keep their
   committed path to the rim. Walk off a rear deck without jumping and
   check that joining players see a continuous fall, without a down/up dip.
   Repeat those actions as a joining client, not only on the host.
   Turn and tackle quickly, then catch a moving teammate's pass. A catch
   should keep the ball, and the tackle should follow the new direction.
   Sprint, reverse the stick/key direction, and watch the feet scramble
   while momentum slows before reversing. Repeat diagonally and on the
   joining PC. Check that the commitment feels useful rather than delayed.
   Hold a shot, get tackled, then catch again without releasing shoot.
   Releasing now should keep the ball; a fresh press should start a new shot.
   Orbit behind the hoop while holding the ball. Foreground rim/net should
   fade enough to see you and the ball, without hiding the whole basket.
   Try a long pass while the receiver runs sideways, then a charged lob
   from your own half. Aim dots should stop at the first physical obstruction.
   Jump a rotating sweeper, then deliberately hit it while carrying the ball.
   Choose a different Hype power each round. Check ready/active feedback,
   Giant crossing sweepers, deployed pad launches and homing-ball impacts.
   Finish a teammate's pass quickly with a basket and check assist Hype.

2. Complete a match. Check that everybody sees the same score and winner.
   Winning humans get one bag each. Open and equip an item, then rematch.
   Check the other players can see the equipped item and no extra reward
   arrives for the same finished round.

3. Host Custom teams. Put two people on one side and one on the other.
   Balance teams with bots ON: the lone person gets a bot teammate (2v2).
   Turn it OFF for a 2v1 match. With two connected people, try 1v1.

4. Have one client leave and rejoin during a match. A bot takes over the
   vacant seat. Check that the returning player can move and touch the ball.
   Closing the host should return the clients to their menu with a message.

WHAT STILL NEEDS HUMAN FEEDBACK
- Do jumping, camera movement, catching, shooting and dunk timing feel good?
- Does the ball stay in the hands during a boosted jump on joining PCs?
- Does ball camera keep a high ball visible, and release when you look?
- Is the larger court fast to cross using boosts without becoming empty?
- Is the game smooth on all three PCs over your actual internet path?
- Are tackles fair, bots useful, and both teams' hoops easy to read?
- Do bots offer useful passing lanes? Does the named pass target match intent?
- Can you track the paneled ball through crowded catches and dunks?
- Are the new impacts clear and comfortable, with quiet footsteps and no
  harsh/repetitive tones? Test headphones and speakers at normal volume.
- Do your physical controllers work as expected?
- Does unplugging during a charged shot cancel it and open pause?
- After reconnecting, can you resume without a stuck action or drift?

Write down the action, which PC was hosting, and what went wrong.
The automated checks cover rules and synchronization, not human enjoyment
or the particular network between your three machines.
