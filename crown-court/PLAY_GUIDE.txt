CROWN COURT 0.12.0 - FAMILY REVIEW

FAMILY CHARACTERS
Wardrobe > Character: Fresh Bean, Evie, Tyler or Dad.
Then equip any owned clothes and accessory. Your unlocks and bags stay saved.
Character art is still under review. This build has not been published.

START
Run CrownCourt.exe. It contains the game and assets.
Use CrownCourt32.exe if Windows rejects the 64-bit build.
Practice and LAN need no account. Everyone must use version 0.12.0 for this review build.
Internet rooms await public service deployment. See INTERNET_PLAY.txt.
Enter your name, choose a mode, bot difficulty and Hype power, then Practice, Host, or Join.
Hype choices: Giant, Jump pad, or Homing ball. Q/Y uses it when full.
The meter always names your power and shows its charge and active duration.
Passes, catches, tackles, assists and baskets earn Hype.
Giant lasts 7 seconds; a placed jump pad lasts 14 seconds.
The homing ball pursues an opponent and knocks them down on an unguarded hit.

The 64x112 court has winding raised roads, low turf pockets, four launch pads
and four speed strips. Use a flank road or cut through a low goal approach.
Jump over the four rotating padded sweepers; contact can knock the ball loose.
Giant can stride through them. Loose balls physically rebound off the bars.
Easy bots are the default; Normal and Hard react faster and aim better.
Passes lead running teammates. Shooting follows your aim and look angle.
Aim with the trajectory dots. More shot charge reaches farther;
try a lob from your own half. Aim dots stop at the first static obstruction.

MODES
3 vs bots: 1-3 humans together; bots fill the team for 3v3.
Custom teams: 2 humans default to 1v1; 3 humans default to 2v1.
  The host chooses sides. "Balance teams with bots" adds one bot to
  the lone player's side in 2v1. Turn it off to play without that bot.
Free-for-all: 3 scoring players; empty seats use bots.

Team games: 5 minutes, or a 4-point lead.
Free-for-all: 3 minutes, or first to 7.
Teams launch from raised decks after the 3-second countdown. Use the extra jump in flight. Every basket or dunk is 1 point.
The clock stops after a basket while players reset.
Ties continue until the next score.

KEYBOARD / MOUSE
WASD             Move
Mouse            Look
Space twice      Jump, then double jump
Left mouse       Catch without ball / pass while holding
Right mouse      Tackle without ball / hold and release to shoot
F                Dunk near the hoop while airborne
E                Catch
Shift            Tackle
Ctrl             Toggle ball camera
R                Recenter camera
Q                Use Hype power when its meter is full
Middle mouse     Request a pass
F1 / Esc         Controls / pause menu
F11              Fullscreen
M                Sound on/off

CONTROLLER
Left/right stick Move/look
A / Cross twice  Jump, then double jump
X / Square       Catch/pass
RT / R2          Tackle/hold and release to shoot
B / Circle       Dunk
RB / R1          Request a pass
LB / L1          Ball camera
Right stick press Recenter
Y / Triangle     Hype power
Start / Options  Pause menu

Menus: D-pad/left stick to move, A/Cross to confirm, B/Circle to go back.
Nintendo prompts use B/A/Y/X in their physical positions. Unknown pads
use Bottom/Right/Left/Top labels. Follow the prompts shown for your pad.
Select Name, Host address or Room code for the controller keyboard.
The first available controller is used until disconnected. Disconnecting
opens pause and cancels a held shot. Release controls before resuming.
Release a shot before the meter turns red; overcharging makes it overshoot.
Dunking uses F/B, not the shoot button.
Passing is for team modes. Aim at a teammate: the prompt names your target.
Out of range or behind you? Turn toward them; the ball stays in your hands.
The Extra jump hint means you still have one jump available in the air.
Practice pauses; a network match keeps running while your menu is open.
Move the mouse or right stick to release ball camera.
Changing direction preserves momentum briefly. Plant the turn before you
jump; you carry momentum into the jump. Small stick movements
still let you walk and make fine adjustments.
E and left click accept a catch pressed just before the ball arrives.
Chevron strips boost speed; round pads launch you upward. Steer in the
air and use the extra jump to extend the arc. Near the hoop, jump then
press F/B; a slightly early dunk press is remembered. The dunk finishes
before the court resets after a score.

LAN
Use the same Wi-Fi or Ethernet network on all PCs.
Run version 0.11.0 on every PC.
1. One PC chooses Host.
2. Other PCs enter the IPv4 shown in that lobby and choose Join.
3. The host chooses Start.

Use the host's current address shown in the lobby.
Port: UDP 28888. If Windows Firewall asks, allow Private networks.
Players can join during a match. Bots take over disconnected seats.
The host starts rematches. Closing the host ends the session.

INTERNET
Internet rooms require the approved public service to be deployed first.
This local build leaves that service unconfigured. Practice and LAN work.
Once enabled: Host room, copy its code, friends choose Join room.
No extra app or router forwarding. See INTERNET_PLAY.txt.

WARDROBE
Start with a blank outfit and no accessory. Winning humans present at
the final whistle get 1 free bag each. Losing players get no bag.
Open bags after a win or in Wardrobe. Equip the item or keep it for later.
There are 11 cosmetic unlocks; they do not change stats. No purchases.
Rarity chances start at 50% Common / 30% Rare / 15% Epic / 5% Legendary.
Completed tiers are rerolled. No duplicate items before the collection
is complete. Bags are kept when all items have been collected.
If a reward save fails, keep the app open while fixing the save-folder
problem. Saving retries automatically, even from the menu.

SAVE LOCATION
%APPDATA%\CrownCourt\closet.json  (wardrobe and bags)
%APPDATA%\CrownCourt\settings.cfg (name, host address, Hype power, sound)
Saves are local to this Windows user. Copy that folder to move them.
